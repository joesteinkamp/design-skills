# Performance Review Template

> **Field Definitions** — use for output validation.
>
> | Field | Required | Type | Validation |
> |-------|----------|------|------------|
> | Designer | yes | text | name or identifier |
> | Role | yes | text | current role title |
> | Level | yes | text | current level, e.g. mid / senior / staff / manager |
> | Review period | yes | text | the cycle being assessed, e.g. H1 2026 |
> | Cycle type | yes | enum | annual / mid-year / promo-case |
> | Inputs used | yes | list | which sources were available: self-assessment / peer feedback / manager notes / prior goals |
> | Rating scale | yes | enum | default / custom |
> | Overall read | yes | text | 2-3 sentences against the current level |
> | Overall rating | yes | enum | 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 |
> | Promotion readiness | yes | enum | ready / on-track / not-yet / n-a |
> | Goal Attainment | yes | list | one row per prior goal; states absence if none supplied |
> | Goal status | yes | enum | met / partial / missed / descoped |
> | Competency Scorecard | yes | list | one row per rubric dimension |
> | Dimension | yes | text | rubric dimension name |
> | Rating | yes | enum | 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 or not-yet-evidenced |
> | Level vs expectation | yes | enum | meets / exceeds / below / not-yet-evidenced |
> | Evidence | yes | text | specific reference to work or behavior in the period |
> | Strengths | yes | list | min 2, each with supporting evidence |
> | Growth Areas | yes | list | min 2, each with why it matters at the level |
> | Growth Plan | yes | list | min 1 per growth area; specific, ownable actions plus suggested goal themes |
> | Open Questions | yes | list | min 1; unresolved items, evidence gaps, confidence caveats |
