# Performance Rubric

Use this rubric to rate a designer's performance systematically across competency dimensions. Every rating is **calibrated to the designer's current level** — "Meets expectations" means meeting the bar *for that level*, not in the absolute. Rate against evidence from the review period only.

## Rating Scale

- **1 — Below expectations:** Consistently falls short of the level's bar; needs significant support.
- **2 — Inconsistently meets:** Meets the bar in some areas but unevenly; gaps recur.
- **3 — Meets expectations:** Reliably meets the level's bar; solid, dependable contribution.
- **4 — Exceeds:** Regularly operates above the level's bar; takes on scope beyond it.
- **5 — Outstanding:** Operates well beyond the level, with broad impact; a model for the level above.
- **Not-yet-evidenced:** No evidence available this cycle — mark as such rather than rating low by default.

## Competency Dimensions

### 1. Craft & quality
- Quality, polish, and rigor of design output relative to the level.
- Anchors: *Meets* — output is consistently usable, on-system, and ships without major rework. *Exceeds* — raises the craft bar for peers; output is a reference others reuse.

### 2. Problem framing & strategy
- Frames the right problem, connects work to user and business goals, weighs tradeoffs.
- Anchors: *Meets* — reframes ambiguous asks into clear problems within their scope. *Exceeds* — shapes strategy beyond assigned scope; influences what the team works on.

### 3. Impact & outcomes
- Measurable results: user, product, and business outcomes the work produced.
- Anchors: *Meets* — work ships and moves the metrics it set out to. *Exceeds* — drives outcomes that change team or org direction.

### 4. Collaboration & influence
- Works effectively with PM, engineering, research, and peers; builds trust and alignment.
- Anchors: *Meets* — a reliable partner; resolves disagreement constructively. *Exceeds* — aligns groups beyond their team; mentors and elevates others.

### 5. Communication & storytelling
- Clarity of written, verbal, and visual communication; makes the work and rationale legible.
- Anchors: *Meets* — communicates decisions and rationale clearly to the immediate team. *Exceeds* — tells a compelling narrative to leadership and across functions.

### 6. Autonomy & ownership
- Operates independently, drives work to done, and owns outcomes including the messy parts.
- Anchors: *Meets* — owns their projects end-to-end with light guidance. *Exceeds* — takes ownership of ambiguous, cross-team problems no one else is holding.

### 7. Growth & receptiveness to feedback
- Seeks and acts on feedback; develops skills; reflects and adjusts.
- Anchors: *Meets* — integrates feedback and visibly improves over the cycle. *Exceeds* — actively grows others and builds team learning practices.

## Calibration Guidance

- **Differentiate.** A healthy scorecard shows a spread, not a flat row of 3s or 4s.
- **Evidence first.** Each rating must trace to specific work or behavior in the period.
- **Level, not person.** Rate the work against the level's bar; avoid judgments about personality, potential, or traits unsupported by evidence.
- **Guard against bias.** Weigh the whole period, not just recent events; check that ratings are not driven by a single memorable project, halo, or horn effect.
